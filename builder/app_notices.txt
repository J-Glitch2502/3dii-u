Third-party software in 3Dii U Builder
======================================

3Dii U Builder is free and open-source under the European Union Public Licence v1.2 (LICENSE.txt, beside this file). Its
source is at https://github.com/josiahg2502-glitch/3dii-u and in this app's engine folder.

It carries the programs and libraries below, each under its own licence. THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md, beside this file, has
every notice and the full text of every licence. Each GitHub release also offers the source of the GPL and LGPL parts.

3dsrecomp by Nathan Soufflet (nathsou), EUPL-1.2: the recompiler and runtime the builder is built on, with this project's changes
  engine\third_party\3dsrecomp, with its source: https://github.com/nathsou/3dsrecomp

devkitPro: devkitPPC (GCC 16.2.0 and Binutils 2.47, GNU GPL v3 or later; newlib 4.6.0, BSD-style), wut 1.9.2 (zlib licence),
wut-tools 1.3.5 (GNU GPL v2 or later). GCC's runtime libraries carry the GCC Runtime Library Exception.
  engine\devkitpro, licence texts in its licenses folders: https://devkitpro.org/ and https://github.com/devkitPro
  The folder also holds devkitPro packages the build doesn't use (libogc2, SDL2, portlibs and others), each with its licence.

CafeGLSL 1.0.0-rc1 by Exzap (MIT), built on Mesa (MIT and others)
  engine\third_party\cafeglsl and engine\third_party\cafeglsl_ppc: https://github.com/Exzap/CafeGLSL
MinGW-w64 winpthreads (MIT and BSD-style) and zlib (zlib licence), with CafeGLSL's Windows build

FFmpeg 4.4's libavcodec and libavutil, AAC decoder only (GNU LGPL v2.1 or later)
  engine\toolchain\aac_ppc: https://ffmpeg.org/

makerom 0.19.0 from Project_CTR by 3DSGuy, applestash and Jakcron (MIT), with Mbed TLS (Apache 2.0) and libyaml (MIT)
  _internal\bin\makerom.exe: https://github.com/3DSGuy/Project_CTR

Python 3.14 (Python Software Foundation License), with OpenSSL 3 (Apache 2.0) and the libraries Python ships
Qt 6.12 and PySide6 / Shiboken6 6.12 (GNU LGPL v3), as separate DLLs that can be replaced with other builds
pyctr (MIT), PyCryptodome (BSD 2-Clause and public domain), cryptography (Apache 2.0 or BSD 3-Clause), cffi and setuptools (MIT)
PyInstaller bootloader (GNU GPL v2 or later, with its exception for bundled programs)
Microsoft Visual C++ and Universal C runtime files, redistributed with Python under Microsoft's terms
  all in _internal

The boot movie every game's title carries comes from shinyquagsire23's woomïnstaller (GNU LGPL v2.1).

3Dii U is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo. It is for games and system files you dumped
yourself from consoles you own, and for homebrew.
