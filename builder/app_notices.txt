Third-party software in 3Dii U Builder
======================================

3Dii U Builder carries the programs and libraries below. Each is under its own licence. Where a licence text ships with this copy, the folder is named; the links lead to each project and its source code.

3dsrecomp by nathsou (European Union Public Licence v1.2), the recompiler and runtime the builder is built on, with this project's changes
  engine\third_party\3dsrecomp, its source as it ships here, licence text in engine\third_party\3dsrecomp\LICENSE: https://github.com/nathsou/3dsrecomp

Python 3.14 (Python Software Foundation License)
  _internal: https://www.python.org/

Qt 6 and PySide6 / Shiboken6 (GNU LGPL v3)
  _internal\PySide6 and _internal\shiboken6, as separate DLLs that can be replaced with other builds: https://www.qt.io/ and https://code.qt.io/

PyInstaller bootloader (GNU GPL v2, with its exception for bundled programs)
  3Dii U Builder.exe: https://pyinstaller.org/

pyctr (MIT), PyCryptodome (BSD 2-Clause and public domain), cryptography (Apache 2.0 or BSD 3-Clause)
  _internal: https://github.com/ihaveamac/pyctr, https://www.pycryptodome.org/, https://cryptography.io/

makerom v0.19.0, from Project_CTR by 3DSGuy (MIT)
  _internal\bin\makerom.exe: https://github.com/3DSGuy/Project_CTR

devkitPro: devkitPPC (GCC and Binutils, GNU GPL v3; newlib, BSD-style licences), wut and their tools
  engine\devkitpro, licence texts in engine\devkitpro\licenses: https://devkitpro.org/ and https://github.com/devkitPro

CafeGLSL by Exzap (MIT)
  engine\third_party\cafeglsl and engine\third_party\cafeglsl_ppc (licence text in its folder): https://github.com/Exzap/CafeGLSL

MinGW-w64 winpthreads (MIT and BSD-style) and zlib (zlib licence)
  engine\third_party\cafeglsl: https://www.mingw-w64.org/ and https://zlib.net/

FFmpeg 4.4's libavcodec and libavutil, built with only the AAC decoder (GNU LGPL v2.1 or later)
  engine\toolchain\aac_ppc: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

The source code of the GPL and LGPL parts, as they ship here, is available on request from whoever gave you this copy.
